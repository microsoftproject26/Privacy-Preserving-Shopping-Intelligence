# Realistic downstream plan — Fable 5.1 (verbatim; received 2026-09-27; for the owner's decision)
Builds on company/plans/DOWNSTREAM_REALISTIC_PLAN_DRAFT.md and PREG1_AMENDMENT_03. Status: PROPOSED — awaiting owner approval.

(1) Arms → scenarios. Scenario 1: C_BIG (P3 winner runs) and C_SMALL. Scenario 2: FA and FP at SMALL. Scenario 3: PF and CPF are NOT the owner's scenario — per EVALUATION_CONTRACT E11.9a they adapt only a personal component p_u (initialised 0); full local-model adaptation is registered for LO only. So add two arms: FT_C (C_SMALL shipped, then the whole SMALL model fine-tuned on the client's own data) and FT_FA (the FA model, then fine-tuned). Keep PF/CPF as the cheap-personalisation tier and CPF as PF's capacity-matched control. Scenario 3b: LO. Losses: C_BIG→C_SMALL (size), C_SMALL→FA/FP (federation), FA→FT_FA and C_SMALL→FT_C (personalisation gain), LO (collaboration).

(2) Realism knobs (registered values). Participation: 64 clients per round, sampled uniformly without replacement, 3 visits per client. Drop-out: 10% of sampled clients per round report nothing (seeded, identical for FA/FP/PF); aggregation weighted by n_consumed; rounds = 6,827 (= 3×131,072/64 ÷ 0.9) so expected consumed exposures stay at 6.0 EFE — measured and reported. Local work: B 16, 2 passes (ADR-005); local LR from the registered {peak, 0.5×peak} grid on the SMALL peak; FedProx μ = 0.01, single pre-registered value, disclosed as untuned (Li et al. 2020, MLSys). Personalisation budget (FT_C, FT_FA, LO alike): passes ∈ {1, 2, 4}, LR ∈ {0.1, 0.3}× the SMALL peak, B 16, no early stopping; selected once on M04S FIXED_20 before any outcome (E11.9a). Support: warm clients = their own TRAIN examples (target_ts < TRAIN_END); cold/U_NEW = E11.3 support labels inside the permitted history with targets strictly before decision_ts(q); query targets never feed back (E11.9); no prequential variant. Causal by construction, tested by the existing golden replay (T13/T41).

(3) KD C_BIG→SMALL: real value (Tang & Wang 2018, KDD: compact rankers recover most teacher quality) but new trainer code, a teacher-scoring pass over 2.59M rows (~10 min), one SMALL training (≈ 3 GPU-h) — and it only changes scenario 3's general model. Register it as CONDITIONAL: run only if the measured C_BIG→C_SMALL drop exceeds the threshold; never on the critical path.

(4) Metrics beyond accuracy: model size FP32 (and INT8 estimate), per-visit down+up bytes, total bytes per client and fleet, rounds, consumed EFE, local updates per client, per-client adaptation time on the A16 (proxy, disclosed), support labels per client. Reporting: client-macro and micro MRR@20 per arm; per stratum (n_train quantiles, SPARSE/HEAVY panels, WARM_TRUNCATION M04W, TRUE_COLD/COLD_H M04), U_NEW Study A/B, events-to-90/95%, and the share of clients HARMED by personalisation (Yu, Bagdasaryan & Shmatikov 2020: many clients gain nothing without local adaptation).

(5) Cost (INFERENCE, ±30%, 2 A16): matrix at SMALL ≈ 58 GPU-h + 11% drop-out extension ≈ 63; C_SMALL mini-ladder/θ0 ≈ 2; FT recipe selection on M04S ≈ 9; FT_C + FT_FA, 2 seeds, evaluated on the panels + U_NEW_VAL (≈ 62k clients) ≈ 20–40 (per-client FT time unmeasured — SYS-05 bench first; fallback order if over budget: HEAVY then SPARSE to 8,192); KD ≈ 4 if triggered. Total ≈ 95–115 GPU-h ≈ 2.5–3 days wall. Note: the existing Study B adaptation evaluations were never in SYS-02 either.

(6) Register NOW (before any P2/P3 read): arm list incl. FT_C/FT_FA, all knob values above, the FT grid and selection rule, support/causality rules, panel and metric lists, KD condition, fallback order. After G2: SMALL recipe (mini-ladder), absolute local/FT LRs (fractions are registered now), SYS-04c/SYS-05 benches, FL-03/TRAIN-05 code (drop-out sampling, full-model FT, KD) under Opus gates.

(7) Threshold (owner decides): relative drop of client-macro MRR@20 (2-seed mean) ≤ 2% point AND UB95 ≤ 3% (paired user bootstrap, MAIN TEST at ALL_METHODS_LOCK) for C_BIG→C_SMALL, C_BIG→FA/FP and C_BIG→FT_FA (the deployed on-device system). Personalisation gains carry CIs, no threshold; harmed-client share reported.
