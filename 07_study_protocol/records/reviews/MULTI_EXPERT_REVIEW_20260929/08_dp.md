## 1. What the project does (MEASURED)

### 1.1 Mechanism (`code/fl/dp.py`)
- **One round:** the server computes θ_{r+1}[k] = θ_r[k] + (Σ_{u∈S_r} clip_S(δ_u)[k] + z·S·ξ_r[k]) / m. The clip is a flat L2
  clip over all shared tensors, clip factor = min(1, S/‖δ_u‖). The sum is unweighted. ξ_r ~ N(0, I). The denominator m is
  **fixed** at 1,024 and is never the survivor count (`fl/dp.py:5-11`, `:77-80`, `:122-130`, `:179-192`). This is the
  fixed-denominator estimator f̃_f of McMahan et al. 2018.
- **Norms:** each client's norm is computed from the FP32 delta, reduced in float64 (`:68-74`).
- **Noise source:** a CPU `torch.Generator` seeded by `derive_seed(seed, "dp_noise", r)`, in FP32 (`:157-165`). The seed is
  public and deterministic, and the PRNG is not cryptographic. This is disclosed in 06E §9(b).
- **No data-dependent side outputs:** with z > 0 the round returns no per-client norm and no clipped-client count
  (`:201-206`). Norms are returned only in the calibration mode (clip_norm=None, z=0). FA_1024 (z=0 with clipping) returns
  only the clipped-client count (`:204-205`).
- **Empty Poisson rounds:** an empty round releases noise only (`:170-174`; `fl/runtime.py:552-554`, `:584-594`).
- **S rule:** S = the median pre-clip norm over rounds 1–20 (round_idx 0..19) of an unclipped FA_1024-config pass at seed
  2026 (`fl/dp.py:210-229`; amendment 06 §2 Q3; `v4int/launch_fl.py:579-604`). The S record is labelled "non-private;
  follows the unclipped trajectory".

### 1.2 Sampling (`code/fl/participation.py`, amendment 06C)
- **Poisson sampling:** every client joins round r independently with probability q = m/N = 1,024/131,072 = 2⁻⁷. The draw is
  u_i ~ Uniform{0..N−1} from `PCG64(derive_seed(seed,"poisson",r))`, and client i is a member iff u_i < m. The inclusion
  probability is exact (`:19-26`, `:84-91`, `:155-158`).
- **Guards:** every DP-configured run (DP, FA_1024, S_CAL) must use Poisson sampling with group_size == m and dropout_p = 0
  (`fl/runtime.py:235-240`, `:409-419`). DP is allowed for method FA only.
- **Schedule and marks are data-independent:** the LR schedule and the 12 evaluation marks depend on the round index only
  (`fl/runtime.py:42-48`, `:527-529`).
- **Why the switch:** the earlier sampler was fixed-size sampling without replacement (WOR), and the switch to Poisson was
  made because of the FL-06 WOR receipt (06C L7-17).

### 1.3 Accountant (`code/fl/accountant.py`)
- **One step:** RDP of the Poisson-subsampled Gaussian (Mironov, Talwar & Zhang 2019). Integer orders use the exact binomial
  expansion (`:92-101`). Fractional orders use the MTZ series (`:104-136`).
- **Composition and conversion:** RDP composes as T·RDP (`:159-163`). The registered conversion is the "standard" one,
  ε = RDP + log(1/δ)/(α−1) (`:167-176`, `:47`). The "improved" (Balle et al. 2020) value is reported for information only.
- **z search and rounding:** z is found by bisection and returned from the safe side, then rounded UP to 4 dp
  (`:214-256`).
- **Registered values** (`code/tests_fl/receipts/DP_Z_RECORD_FL04.json`):
  - z(ε=8) = 0.5642: achieved ε 7.99825, α* 2.9, improved conversion 7.015.
  - z(ε=1) = 1.3094: achieved ε 0.99985, α* 16, improved conversion 0.7505.
  - Both at m 1,024, N 131,072, T 384, δ 5e-6.
  - z(ε=4) = 0.7244 is in the FL-06 receipt (`DP_WOR_SENSITIVITY_FL06.json` "eps4_z").
- **Launcher pins:** the launcher accepts only ε ∈ {8, 1} and checks the record against
  `DP_ACCOUNTING = {m 1024, N 131072, T 384, δ 5e-6}` (`v4int/launch_fl.py:166-168`, `:563-577`).
- **Stale text:** the module docstring and the record's `sampling_disclosure` still describe fixed-size WOR sampling
  (`accountant.py:25-31`, `:252-255`). This is known (REV-FL-01 D1) and superseded by 06E §9(a).

## 3. Comparison with the state of the art

### 3.1 Strengths (MEASURED + OPINION)
- **S1 — The mechanism and accountant are textbook-correct, and I re-derived them independently (§1.7).** The pieces:
  - fixed denominator (the right estimator under Poisson sampling, L2);
  - user-level add/remove adjacency;
  - z rounded up (safe side);
  - the conservative standard conversion registered, with the improved conversion reported;
  - δ < 1/N;
  - a 0-member round releases noise, so round existence does not leak;
  - no per-client statistic is released when z > 0;
  - LR schedule and evaluation marks depend on the round index only.
  This is better hygiene than many published FL-DP simulations.
- **S2 — FL-06/06C caught a real, published class of accounting error before any DP run.** Accounting a fixed-size WOR
  sampler with the Poisson bound is the error L6 and L7 warn against, and the fix (Poisson sampling) is the right one.
- **S3 — The confirmatory DP contrasts avoid the private checkpoint choice.** K12/K13 are confirmatory only at
  CONTROLLED_BUDGET (06D §3), which is exactly the private-selection issue L13 addresses. Pairing FA_1024 (z = 0, same S,
  same Poisson cohorts through the same seed stream, REV-FL-01 §1) with DP isolates the noise cost as common random numbers.
- **S4 — The noise-to-signal note (06D §3) and the scale interpretation (06I §6b) were registered before any DP value
  exists.** A large loss can therefore be read as a design property, not an excuse after the fact.
- **S5 — The disclosure list already names S, the LR multiplier, the pooled block, PB selection, the public PRNG seed and
  "ε covers θ only".** An examiner will find most caveats written down.
- **S6 — A realistic-scale point (DP8_FULL) and a public-pretraining point (DP8_FROZEN) are both registered.** Their tiers
  are the problem (§3.4).

### 3.2 Weaknesses / errors (numbered W; INFERENCE unless stated)

**W1 — A fixed clip S combined with a decaying LR schedule (HIGH; conditional on the S3 shape).**
- The noise z·S/m per coordinate is **constant in r** (`fl/dp.py:11`). The client delta scales with the round's local LR
  (lr = peak·s(r), `fl/runtime.py:537`). S is calibrated on rounds 1–20, which are at peak LR under both S0 and FL_CONST.
- Under S0 (`fl/schedule.py:38`) the LR is ≤ 0.1× peak for about the last 50 % of the 384 rounds. In those rounds the deltas
  are about 10× below S, so clipping never binds and the signal falls 10–100× while the noise does not. That half of
  training adds noise with little signal.
- The **confirmatory** K12/K13 release is round 384, after the 0.01× cooldown, so it is the most noise-dominated checkpoint
  of the run. Compare DP-SGD (L1), where noise is added before the LR and decays with it. McMahan 2018 used a constant client
  LR and warns that the clip must be re-evaluated when the LR changes (L2, footnote 4). Adaptive clipping (L8) exists for
  exactly this reason.
- **Result:** the measured ε = 8 cost would partly be an artefact of the schedule, not of privacy. Under FL_CONST only the
  last 10 % of rounds are affected, so the problem is milder.
- **A zero-privacy-cost fix exists.** The clip S_r = S·f(r), with f the registered round LR factor, is data-independent, so
  the accountant is unchanged (ε depends on z, q, T, δ only; `accountant.py:200-204`). The noise z·S_r then decays with the
  LR, as it does in DP-SGD.
- **A free diagnostic already exists.** FA_1024 logs the per-round clipped-client count (`fl/dp.py:204-205`), so the
  clipped fraction by round can be reported and shows directly whether W1 occurs.

**W2 — Server momentum or an adaptive server optimiser under DP is covered only for FedAdam (HIGH, registration gap).**
- 06H §3 triggers DP_OPT_SENS only "if the 06G joint selection picks FedAdam". 06J later added **FedAvgM** (β 0.9, η 1)
  to S2 and did not extend 06H.
- With β = 0.9, θ_T − θ_0 = η Σ_r (1 − β^{T−r+1})/(1 − β) Δ_r ≈ 10·Σ_r Δ_r. That is, FedAvgM at η = 1 is FedAvg at an
  effective server LR of about 10 **for the noise as well**: the accumulated per-coordinate noise is about 0.108 S instead of
  0.0108 S at ε = 8. The per-round SNR is unchanged, but the step size was chosen on non-DP 64-client runs, where more
  movement helps.
- Production DP-FL does use server momentum (L10), but it tunes the server LR under the noise.
- K12 stays internally valid (optimiser-matched with FA_1024), but "privacy cost" would again be partly "optimiser cost",
  which is the 06H §2 argument applied to FedAvgM.

**W3 — The DP arms inherit a recipe tuned without noise, at m = 64, on CALIB ⊂ MAIN (MEDIUM).**
- Nothing about the DP arm is tuned under noise: no server LR, no clip quantile, no cohort size. DP practice (L12; L10)
  tunes at least the server LR and clip under DP, ideally on public or proxy data or with accounted tuning (L13).
- The consequence is a likely **over-statement** of the privacy cost. It is not a validity error, but the thesis must say
  "the cost of DP at the non-private recipe".
- The project has 1.2 M non-MAIN users (TIEBREAK band minus MAIN). A DP-specific server-LR sensitivity on a disjoint user
  band costs MAIN users no privacy (§4, DP-6).

**W4 — The model is almost entirely item tables, and a fresh AdamW with a full-K softmax makes deltas dense (HIGH for utility
interpretation; MEASURED components).**
- About 98 % of d is the two item tables (REALISM_AUDIT B2). The output-layer gradient of a full-K softmax is dense over all
  158,486 rows. With fresh AdamW the first steps are ≈ lr·sign(g) on every coordinate that has a gradient (`fl/client.py:6`,
  audit B7), so a client delta's L2 norm is dominated by roughly ±lr sign patterns on about 10.1 M output coordinates.
- Clipping to the median norm then bounds a vector whose useful part is small, while the noise is isotropic over all
  20.7 M coordinates.
- Client SGD, if S3 selects it, puts the delta on target and high-probability rows, which is far better for DP (L18/L19
  explain why sparse embedding updates are the crux for DP recommenders).
- So S3's non-DP choice of client optimiser largely decides the DP result. This must be stated with K12/K13. An encoder-only
  federated DP arm (DP8_FROZEN) removes the problem: d ≈ 0.4 M and noise/S ≈ 0.35 (§1.7).

**W5 — The scope of the claim is stated too broadly in the ladder (MEDIUM, wording).**
- 06B §3 L3 says "user-level (ε, δ = 5e-6)-DP of the released model". The precise statement is narrower in five ways:
  - **(a) Central DP with a trusted aggregator.** There is no SecAgg (C5.6(b); `accountant.py:34`). The server sees every
    clipped delta, and therefore each participant's item set (audit B2/B17). DP here protects against anyone who sees the
    released or broadcast models θ_1..θ_384 (every client receives θ_r, and all 384 are accounted). It does **not** protect
    against the aggregator. It is not local DP (compare L21) and not distributed DP (L11).
  - **(b) The privacy unit is a client key (one device-profile), not a person.** A person with several devices or profiles
    gets group privacy only (OPINION; the REES46 user id is a browser-cookie-level identity — UNVERIFIED detail).
  - **(c) Add/remove adjacency only.** Under replace-one the same mechanism gives about 1,132 by RDP (§1.7), or
    (16, (1 + e⁸)·5e-6 ≈ 1.5e-2) by group privacy. That δ is meaningless, so "ε = 8" must never be read as a
    substitution guarantee.
  - **(d) Conditional on the pooled bootstrap block being public.** The block includes the catalogue (support ≥ 5 on full
    TRAIN), vocabularies, the normalisers, the item first-seen anchor and metadata recovery (CLIENT_CONTRACT §6 items 2–6).
    34 of the 53 channels depend on other users' pooled interactions (CLIENT_CONTRACT §6). If a user is removed, the block
    changes: for example, an item at exactly 5 supporters leaves the catalogue and changes the model dimension. Other users'
    inputs change too. So the sensitivity-S argument holds only **given** the block. 06D §3 lists the block as "not
    accounted"; the thesis sentence must say "(ε, δ)-DP of the training mechanism conditional on the public bootstrap block".
  - **(e) The list of unaccounted choices is out of date.** Besides the LR multiplier, the whole FL_TUNING_SELECTION (server
    optimiser, η_s, τ, LR shape, client optimiser and LR; 06F/06G/06I/06J) was chosen on CALIB ⊂ MAIN validation data. The
    06D §3 list names only the multiplier.

**W6 — The 06C narrative mis-attributes the size of the gap (LOW, wording; COMPUTED).**
- 06C L10-11 and DL 150 say fixed-size sampling would give "≈ 1,388 (lower bound ≈ 1,133), not 8".
- (i) The 1,133 is a floor of RDP analyses on that order grid, not a lower bound on ε. The receipt itself says so
  (`floor_note`).
- (ii) Most of the 1,388 vs 8 difference is the **change of adjacency** (replace-one doubles the sensitivity, so σ = z/2 =
  0.28), not WOR versus Poisson. The Poisson mechanism under replace-one at 2S gives about **1,132.5** by RDP (§1.7), which
  equals the receipt's "Prop.-11 floor".
- The correct reason for 06C is that Poisson/add-remove is the pair the registered accountant analyses exactly. WOR has a
  clean analysis only under replace-one, where ε is huge at small σ. L6 and L7 are the right citations.

**W7 — Poisson sampling is not something a real browser fleet can guarantee (MEDIUM for the Edge framing; DISCLOSE).**
- The MAIN ε relies on amplification by sampling (q = 2⁻⁷). Real devices check in when idle, charging and on Wi-Fi (audit
  B11). A server cannot enforce independent Bernoulli inclusion, which is why Gboard moved to DP-FTRL (L9, L10).
- This deserves one sentence in the DP appendix: the reported ε assumes an idealised sampler.

**W8 — The δ margin is thin (LOW).**
- δ = 5e-6 gives N·δ ≈ 0.66 at MAIN, and 5e-7 gives N·δ ≈ 0.67 at FULL. McMahan used δ = 1/K^1.1 (L2).
- The ε at δ = 1/N^1.1 is 8.38 and at 1e-9 is 12.21 (§1.7). Reporting the ε(δ) curve costs nothing and pre-empts the
  question.

**W9 — The gap between FA and FA_1024/DP spans five things (LOW; already partly disclosed).**
- FA → FA_1024 changes cohort size (64 → 1,024), sampling (sweep → Poisson), rounds (6,827 → 384), weighting (n_consumed
  → unweighted, clipped) and, after DL 219, possibly local passes and batch.
- K12/K13 are unaffected. Any "FA vs DP" statement is not a privacy cost.

**W10 — Missing launch paths (LOW-MEDIUM, INFERENCE from code).**
- `load_z` accepts only ε ∈ {8, 1}, and `DP_ACCOUNTING` pins N = 131,072 (`launch_fl.py:166-168`, `:563-577`).
  `--freeze-item-tables` is refused for any arm but FA_WARM_FROZEN (`:1310-1312`).
- So DP4 (z 0.7244; `contracts/fl/DP_Z_EPS4.json` is not written yet, 06B §4), DP8_FULL (DP_Z_FULL.json not written) and
  DP8_FROZEN have **no executable path** today. Each needs a small code task and review before it can run.

**W11 — Minor nits (DISCLOSE-ONLY).**
- q = m/N and T = 3N/m are computed from N (`participation.py:155-158`). Under add/remove, N is the thing that changes. They
  should be declared public constants (q = 2⁻⁷, T = 384); the numerical effect is O(1/N).
- The clip factor is computed from a float64 norm and applied to an FP32 delta, so the clipped norm can exceed S by about
  one ulp.
- The 12 evaluation marks on MAIN_VALIDATION are non-private outputs of the run. They are research measurements, not
  releases, and already fall under "ε covers θ only" (06E §9b).

### 3.3 Unrealistic choices (OPINION)
- **The d/m ratio at MAIN.** With d = 20.7 M and m = 1,024, the per-round noise norm is about 2.5 S (ε 8) and 5.8 S (ε 1),
  about 11–25× McMahan's 0.23 S. ε = 1 at N = 131 k with 20.7 M parameters is expected to be noise (06D §3 already says so).
- **The rest of the design:** 384 rounds, no DP tuning, a fixed clip under a decaying LR, and no public pretraining.
  Production DP-FL (L10) uses populations of 0.8–16 M, models of 2–6 M parameters, public pretraining, adaptive clipping and
  DP-FTRL.

### 3.4 Is the design "doomed", and is DP8_FULL the right realistic point? (INFERENCE / OPINION)
- **MAIN ε = 8 is not strictly doomed.**
  - The encoder (≈ 0.4 M dense coordinates, a signal aligned across up to 1,024 clients) can learn.
  - Over 384 rounds the signal adds linearly (up to 384 S) while the noise grows as √T (≈ 49 S in norm, 0.0108 S per
    coordinate at η = 1).
  - The item tables are the problem: each tail row gets signal from a handful of clients but noise every round. With a
    full-K ranking over 158,486 items, even small per-coordinate noise on tail output rows pushes random tail items into the
    top-20 (the maximum of 158 k Gaussians is ≈ 4.4σ).
  - **Expected outcome:** a strong loss at ε = 8, dominated by W1/W4 if they are not fixed, and near-noise at ε = 1.
  - **Implication:** the MAIN DP arms answer "what does DP cost at this data scale and model size with this non-DP recipe".
    They do not answer "what can private FL achieve".
- **DP8_FULL is the right scale point.** Noise is ≈ 0.32 S per round (my z = 0.5753), in the McMahan regime. It has two
  gaps:
  - (i) It has no noise-free comparator at FULL scale. R(DP8_FULL/C_FULL) mixes FL cost and DP cost, and R(DP8_FULL/FA)
    mixes populations, cohort and rounds. A z = 0 FA_8192_FULL twin would isolate the noise, but doubles the cost.
  - (ii) It inherits W1–W4.
- **The strongest realistic DP point costs less: DP8_FROZEN**, i.e. public-cohort item tables (PRE, non-MAIN users) with a
  DP-federated encoder.
  - d ≈ 0.4 M gives ≈ 0.35 S per round **at MAIN scale**.
  - It mirrors production (public pretraining, L10, L17) and removes the item-set leak of the upload.
  - It is registered only as NICE, and it has no FA_1024-type comparator.
  - I would rank it at least equal to DP8_FULL.

### 3.5 Missing pieces
- Adaptive or quantile clipping (L8) or an LR-proportional clip (W1).
- Any empirical privacy evidence (L14/L15): even a simple user-level membership-inference test of FA versus DP8, or the
  audit's item-set reconstruction from one non-DP delta (audit B2), would make the privacy ladder concrete.
- SecAgg or distributed DP discussion (L11): in simulation, SecAgg does not change the arithmetic of the sum, so DP-arm
  utility equals what SecAgg + central-noise DP would give. Distributed discrete Gaussian adds only quantisation and a
  dropout noise shortfall (INFERENCE; worth a paragraph).
- A PLD (numerically tight) ε for information. RDP with the standard conversion is loose at α* ≈ 3 (the improved conversion
  already gives 7.02). The size of the PLD gain here is UNVERIFIED.

## 5. Score: **7 / 10**

The engineering and accounting are close to exemplary for an MSc, better than many published DP-FL simulations:
- the mechanism and accountant are correct, and I independently reproduced z(8) = 0.5642, z(1) = 1.3094 and z(4) = 0.7244
  to about 1e-11;
- a real published class of sampling/accountant mismatch was caught and fixed before any run;
- there are no data-dependent side outputs;
- the private checkpoint choice was removed from the confirmatory contrasts;
- the noise-to-signal interpretation was registered before reading.

What keeps the score from 8–9 is the scientific design of the DP arms, not their correctness:
- **W1:** the clip is fixed while the LR schedule decays, so late rounds are noise with little signal, and the confirmatory
  release is the round-384 endpoint.
- **W2:** server-momentum amplification of noise is not covered by the registered sensitivity.
- **W3/W4:** the DP arms inherit a recipe tuned without noise, and the item tables make up almost all of the model.
- **W5:** the guarantee is stated more broadly than it holds (central trust, add/remove only, conditional on the pooled
  block, tuning unaccounted).
- **W10:** the two realistic DP points (FULL, FROZEN) are low-tier or have no launch path.

As registered, the MAIN DP arms will most likely show that "DP destroys utility at this scale and model size". That is
honest, and it was pre-interpreted, but it is weak evidence for the proposal's privacy–utility question unless DP-1/DP-2
and at least one of DP8_FROZEN or DP8_FULL are run. All of these are still registrable, because no DP value exists.
